Questions

What is ZYKLUS?

ZYKLUS — Nothing Was Lost is an illustrated sci-fi audio drama in English. Six million people live inside a mountain, ten levels deep; heat rises, so the poor live at the top and the rich at the bottom. It is adapted from the ZYKLUS novels by Tim Mecklenburg.

Where can I watch it?

Free on YouTube at youtube.com/@thezyklus. A new episode comes out every Friday at 8 pm Berlin time.

Is there a game?

Yes. Locked Out is a free browser game set in the city of Episode 1. Nothing to install; your progress is saved in your browser.

What is BRACKS?

A ZYKLUS spin-off about Erek Bracks: an audio drama every Wednesday at 8 pm from 14 October 2026, and BRACKS Manga, a read-aloud fight manga about his youth in illegal back rooms, every Monday and Friday at 6 pm.

Is ZYKLUS made with AI?

The story and the world are written by a human author. Narration, voices, the faces of the speaking characters, illustrations and music are created with the help of AI, disclosed under Art. 50 of the EU AI Act.

What does Zyklus mean?

Zyklus is German for cycle.
