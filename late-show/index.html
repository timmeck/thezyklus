PIP ARDEN / FIFTY-SIX YEARS LATER

Still here.
Still on the air.

In 2705, Pip was the cadet outside the camera’s frame. In 2761, he has a microphone of his own.

Live from a cramped booth on Mars, he talks with the people who were there — and the people who decided what everyone else would see. There are stories, questions on cards, and an incoming phone line.

The red lamp belongs to the show’s fictional world. The real premiere dates are below.

PULL UP A CHAIR

Good company.
Uncomfortable questions.

Two guests. Two ways of remembering.

EPISODE 01 / THE PICTURE EDITOR

Nell Quill

She used to edit the television pictures for Beam. Precise, brisk, and still delighted by a well-built argument. She was not at the dock; she worked with the camera feed.

Her request: one sentence at the end. Uncut.

EPISODE 02 / THE POD DRIVER

Teo Lark

He drove the school pod on the victory tour. Now about eighty-six, he brings his own cushion and remembers the rescue from the driver’s seat.

He was the last person out.
