STAY UP.
SEND THEM OUT.

Training hits build strain. More strain means a bigger launch. Send the other cadets beyond the arena. A recovery drone brings everyone back. In a timed round, you have 150 seconds. Your ring-outs minus your own recoveries decide the winner. Choose Three recoveries for a last-crew-standing match with a 150-second limit. Ties use ring-outs, then strain and precise hit statistics.

Keyboard crews

Seat 1 WASD move / aim · Space jump · F fire · G special. Mouse aims, left click fires.

Seat 2 Arrow keys move / aim · Right Shift jump · K fire · L special

Seat 3 N / M move · I / comma aim up / down · H jump · Y or Z fire · U special

Seat 4 Numpad 4 / 6 move · 8 / 2 aim up / down · 5 jump · 0 fire · decimal special

Controller & touch

Left stick or D-pad moves. Right stick aims all around. A jumps. X or right trigger fires. B or Y uses the special. Menu pauses. Up to four standard controllers.

On a phone, the MOVE stick steers. Drag AIM / FIRE to aim and keep firing in any direction. Tap to fire forwards. JUMP works again in the air. The thin arrow shows your aim. Controls can be mirrored and resized in settings.

Hold a direction and fire for eight-way keyboard aim. Up aims upwards. Jump has its own key. With no aim input, shots follow your facing direction.

Your cadet course

Begin with Low-g procedures. Read the handbook panel before each fight; its rules decide your grade. Pass each exercise to advance, unlock Pip after lesson 3, and earn the optional Victory Lap after graduation. Failed exercises can be retried. The course and certificates stay on your device.

Make your own trouble

Biscuits ease strain. Gold medals protect briefly. Tape sticks opponents. Gravity buttons make the next jump floatier. A warning announces each arena’s moving parts.

Assistance gives human players a stronger recovery, gentler incoming launches and easier recovery from below the stage. Reduced motion follows your device preference. A tab change pauses the match.
